Make sense of your pet's bloodwork.

Drop your IDEXX VetConnect PLUS PDF reports onto the page and pethud turns them into clear trends over time, plain-language explanations of every value, condition screening, and a head start on your next vet visit — all in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Open the app Free & open source · works offline · your data never leaves your device
Trends at a glance

Every value, every visit — in one dense view

Each analyte becomes a compact sparkline so you can see the direction of travel at a glance. Out-of-range results are flagged and color-coded, and subtle markers show when a test wasn't run or when something in your pet's care changed.

Cited context

Understand what each value means

Open any result for a plain-language, sourced explanation: what the test measures, what a high or low value may suggest, what moves it, how quickly to expect change, and the related metrics to read alongside it. Every statement links to references like Cornell eClinpath, IRIS, and ACVIM/AAFP guidelines.

Condition screening

Disease-oriented panels & IRIS staging

Panels for the conditions that matter in older pets — CKD, hyperthyroidism, heart disease (HCM), IBD / small-cell lymphoma, diabetes and more — gather the relevant markers in one place. For chronic kidney disease, your cat's IRIS stage is computed from its own creatinine and SDMA.

Age-aware vigilance

What's worth watching at your pet's age

A life-stage banner surfaces the conditions to keep an eye on for your pet's current age, split into what bloodwork can screen for versus what to watch clinically — with the guidelines it's drawn from.

Plain-language summaries

What each visit actually means

Every report opens with a readable synthesis: how many values are out of range, what changed since the last visit, and which multi-marker patterns are worth raising — each linking through to the relevant condition panel.

Visit prep

Walk in ready for your vet

pethud turns the latest results into a short prep list: how long it's been since the last panel and the typical recheck cadence, plus specific questions to consider asking — all grounded in your own pet's results.

Your own log

Add the context only you have

Bloodwork makes more sense alongside weight and life events. Record weight and note medications, diet changes or symptoms — weight gets its own trend, and events appear as markers on every chart, so cause and effect are easy to see.

Private by design

Your reports are parsed and stored entirely in your browser using IndexedDB — nothing is ever uploaded to a server. Bring your own PDFs; clear them any time.
